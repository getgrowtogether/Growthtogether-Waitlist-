The free routes — with their limits

Free podcast promotion, route by route

Every route below is free, and together they answer how to promote a podcast for free. Each one also has a ceiling — read it before you spend weeks on the wrong one. One honest note first: the demand figures on this page are tool estimates from 2026-11-15 — "how to promote a podcast" around 780 a month in the US, "how to promote your podcast" around 320 a month — and the ranking pages for these phrases are independent creator sites, not big brands, which is why a genuinely better guide can compete. No volume is claimed as a fact.

1

Guest on other podcasts: borrow trust, don't buy it

Guesting is the fastest free route in podcasting: you appear on a show your future listeners already hear, you answer their real questions well, and a few of them follow you home. Pitch shows one size above yours, not ten sizes above, and pitch a topic their listeners will love — not your show. The honest limit: hosts say no far more than they say yes, and one guest spot brings a handful of listeners, not a flood — it works when you do it again and again.

2

Trailer and promo swaps: two shows, one audience each

Promo swaps are the podcast version of cross-promotion: two shows of a similar size play each other's 30–60 second trailer at the end of an episode. The listener hears a recommendation from a host they already trust. The honest limit: a swap only works when the audiences truly overlap — a true-crime trailer on a cooking show wastes both sides — and finding honest partners by hand does not scale.

3

The 11 free places to list your podcast

Directories and apps list podcasts by category and keep sending listeners long after you submit — the full list with each place's limit is below. The honest limit: a listing is a signpost, not a crowd; the big catalogues hold millions of shows, so a listing alone never makes a show discoverable.

4

Short clips: one strong minute, everywhere your listeners scroll

Cut the best 60 seconds of each episode — the answer, the story, the surprising line — and post it as a clip on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X, with captions, because most people watch muted. The honest limit: a clip is an audition, not the show; most viewers never tap through, and a week of clips buys almost nothing — it is a habit for months.

5

Newsletter swaps: the written version of a trailer swap

Newsletters that cover your topic recommend your show to their readers, and you recommend theirs to your listeners. The named best free tactic for newsletters is the same swap — cross-promotion between people who already have the audience you want. The honest limit: a newsletter mention only works when the readers actually care about your topic; a big list with no overlap sends clicks that never listen.

6

Communities: answer first, mention the show second

Post where your listeners already gather — Reddit communities for your topic, Discord servers, podcast listener groups — and answer a real question well before you ever mention your show. A helpful answer with a show link at the end gets kept; a bare link gets removed. The honest limit: every community has rules against self-promotion, and breaking them once can get you muted there for good.

7

What never works: bought downloads, fake reviews, forced listens

Bought downloads are fake plays from people who never listen — they inflate a number while the real listeners, the ones who finish an episode and come back, stay flat. Fake reviews poison trust with the real listeners you do reach, and forced listens bring people who stop after a minute, which tells every directory your show is not worth recommending. Promotion that lasts is pull: real people who chose to press play.

11 free places to list your podcast

Free podcast promotion sites, with their limits

Submit to all of them — each one is free to list on — but read the limit first, so you know what each listing can and cannot do for you.

1

Apple Podcasts

The biggest podcast catalogue. Free to submit your feed. Limit: millions of shows compete there; a listing alone brings almost no one.

2

Spotify

Where most listeners already have an account and a queue. Free to submit your show. Limit: the app recommends shows people already listen to; new shows start invisible.

3

YouTube

Post the video version or the audio with a still image, and clips find listeners through search. Free to post. Limit: a podcast competes with every video on the platform, not just other podcasts.

4

Podchaser

A podcast database where listeners rate shows and follow creators. Free to claim your show. Limit: a niche audience of podcast lovers, not the general public.

5

Listen Notes

A search engine for podcast episodes — people search a topic and find your episode. Free to claim your show. Limit: only helps listeners who search by topic, and only when your episode titles carry those words.

6

Player FM

A podcast app with topic-based discovery. Free to list. Limit: a smaller audience than the big two catalogues.

7

TuneIn

Radio and podcast directory with a large installed audience. Free to submit. Limit: built for radio first; podcast discovery inside it is weak.

8

Goodpods

A social app where listeners recommend episodes to each other. Free to claim your show. Limit: worth little until real listeners are recommending you there.

9

Podcast Addict

A popular Android podcast app with its own directory. Free to list. Limit: Android only, and a listing is a signpost, not a crowd.

10

Castbox

A podcast app with community features and topic browsing. Free to list. Limit: a smaller audience, and discovery depends on your category.

11

Overcast

A much-loved iPhone podcast app with curated recommendations. Free to be listed once your feed is live. Limit: recommendations are earned, not bought — new shows wait their turn.

A plan you can actually follow

Your first 100 listeners in 14 days

This is how to promote a podcast for free, step by step — how to get your first 100 listeners with no budget and no viral spike: ten real listeners a day, from places where people are already listening.

1–2

Days 1–2: fix the show page and record a trailer

Rewrite the show description with the words listeners type, redo the cover so it reads at thumbnail size, and record a 60-second trailer that plays the best minute of the show — this is what every directory, host and swap partner will judge you on.

3–5

Days 3–5: pitch five guest spots and five swaps

Write one short, honest pitch — the topic you can teach their listeners, in one line — and send it to five shows to guest on and five shows your size for a trailer swap. Submit the show to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Podchaser and Listen Notes the same week.

6–7

Days 6–7: your first guest spot and first swap go live

Record the guest episode, answer the host's questions properly, and let the first trailer swap run. Post the episode in two communities where you have already helped people — not ten places where nobody knows you.

8–14

Days 8–14: one clip a day, one conversation a day

One short clip a day from the best minute of the latest episode, and one honest conversation a day with a listener. At ten listeners a day, the first 100 arrive before the two weeks are over, and they are listeners who chose the show.

Questions people search for

Podcast promotion, answered honestly

How can I promote my podcast for free?

Use the routes that cost time, not money: guest on podcasts your listeners already hear, swap trailers with shows of a similar size, list the show in the 11 free places listeners search, share short clips where your listeners already scroll, and cross-promote with other podcasters. GrowTogether turns cross-promotion into a fair system inside one global circle.

How do I promote my podcast with no budget?

Start with the show itself — a clear title, a description that says who it is for in the first two lines, and a 60-second trailer that shows the best of it — then guest on one show and swap one trailer in the same week, and share one short clip a day for two weeks. Paid ads only speed up a show that already keeps listeners; with no budget, the show has to keep them first.

How do I get my first 100 listeners?

The first 100 listeners almost never come from the directories alone. They come from borrowed trust: a guest appearance on a show your listeners already hear, a trailer swap with a similar-size show, a community post that answers a real question, and other podcasters recommending your show to their listeners. Aim for ten real listeners a day, not one viral spike.

What are the best free podcast promotion sites?

Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, Podchaser, Listen Notes, Player FM, TuneIn, Goodpods, Podcast Addict, Castbox and Overcast all let you list a podcast without paying, and Reddit communities and short clips cost nothing but time. Each one has a limit — a crowded catalogue, a strict audience, or rules against self-promotion — so read the limits on this page before you post.
