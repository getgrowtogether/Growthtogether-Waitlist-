What we collect — and why

GrowTogether is in its waitlist phase. We collect only what the waitlist needs to work, and nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for ads.

1

What you type in the join form

Your email address, your name, your location, the type of your project, and your project link if you choose to give one. The link is optional — you can skip it. We use these to hold your place on the waitlist, to invite you into the beta in order, and to show your project when the circle opens.

2

Your invite link and who used it

Every member gets a personal invite code. We record which code a new signup arrived with, so members who bring genuine builders enter the beta first. Invite records are used only for beta order — never for ranking, and never sold.

3

One record on your own device

Your browser keeps a local copy of your signup, so a returning device sees its original invite link instead of signing up twice. That copy lives on your device, not on our server, and clearing your browser data removes it. The landing page also pings the waitlist service when it loads, so the service is awake when you join — that ping carries no personal information.

4

What we do not collect

No advertising trackers, no cross-site tracking, no payment details — the platform is free and takes no payments — and no passwords. We do not buy data about you from anyone else.

Where it lives

Your data stays in the waitlist

Waitlist records are stored on the waitlist service that runs this site, with routine backups kept by the operator so a service restart cannot erase signups. Records are used only to run the waitlist and the beta — they are never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used to show you ads, because there are no ads on GrowTogether.

1

How long we keep it

For as long as the waitlist and beta run. If the project ever closes, waitlist records are deleted with it.

2

Your choices

You can see your own record at any time from the same device you joined on — your invite link page shows it. To ask for a copy of your record or to have it deleted, contact us at the address published on this site when the beta opens; until then, your record stays in the waitlist only and is never shared. Deletion removes your waitlist place and your invite link.

3

If this policy changes

When the beta opens and the platform starts handling project listings, feedback and discovery sessions, this policy will be updated to cover exactly what is collected then — and the new date will be shown at the top. We will not quietly widen what we collect.

In one line

We collect the minimum, sell nothing, and track nobody for ads

If any sentence on this page ever stops being true, the page — not the practice — is what needs fixing. Tell us through the contact address published here when the beta opens.
