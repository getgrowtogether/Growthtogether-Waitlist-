The service, in plain words

GrowTogether is a free mutual-discovery circle. Right now it is in its waitlist phase: you join the waitlist, get a personal invite link, and are invited into the beta in order. The full circle — submit a project, discover five, earn credits, enter the rotation — opens with the invite-only beta. By joining the waitlist or using the site, you agree to these terms.

1

It is free — and stays free

Joining, submitting a project, discovering others and being discovered cost nothing. There are no plans and no pricing on GrowTogether, and paid priority inside the free rotation will never be sold.

2

You must be old enough to agree where you live

If you are not old enough to agree to terms where you live, please do not join until you are. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

3

The service is offered as it is, while it is being built

The waitlist and the beta are a work in progress. Features, rules and the shape of the circle can change as the beta teaches us what works. We will say so plainly when they do.

Your project

Only list what is honestly yours to show

1

It must be yours — or you must have permission

Submit only projects you built or have the right to promote, and describe them honestly. Copied work, impersonation and misleading descriptions are refused in review.

2

Nothing illegal, deceptive or harmful

No scams, no malware, no phishing, no hate, no content that harms others, and nothing that breaks the law where you live. Listings are reviewed before they appear, and listings that break this rule are removed.

3

Your project stays yours

Listing a project here gives us no ownership of it. You only give us permission to show its name, link and description to members inside the circle.

How members behave

Discover genuinely — or not at all

1

Real attention only

Discover projects by actually opening and looking at them. Bots, fake accounts, auto-surfing, tabs left open to farm credits and click rings are not allowed, and sessions from them are not counted.

2

Never force anyone

Never require another member to install, like, review, subscribe or click an ad. Feedback is welcome when it is honest; pressure is not.

3

One signup per person

Do not create multiple accounts, sign up other people, or use someone else's invite link as your own. One device equals one signup, and fake or self-invites are filtered out of beta order.

4

If you break the rules, you leave the circle

We may remove a listing, remove credits that were farmed, or close an account that breaks these terms — without owing a refund, because there is nothing paid to refund.

What we do — and do not — promise

No promise of customers, sales or rankings

What the circle offers is real people discovering your project and a fair chance to be found. It does not promise customers, sales, signups, a Google ranking, viral reach, or that your project will be shown to any particular person. Discovery depends on your project, on the members discovering it, and on the rotation — not on us guaranteeing an outcome. If these terms change, the new date is shown at the top of this page, and continuing to use the service after a change means you accept the new terms.
