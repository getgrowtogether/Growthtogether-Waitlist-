Community principles

Real discovery — never manufactured numbers

Old traffic exchanges died because people clicked without looking. This circle is built the opposite way: attention and honesty are the currency.

  • A global community where members genuinely explore each other’s work
  • A fair rotation — the same rules for a worldwide brand and a day-one project
  • Honest statistics, labelled exactly as what they are

  • Never bots, auto-surfing or tabs left open to farm credits

Questions

Free promotion, answered honestly

How can I promote my website for free?

Join the waitlist. When the circle opens, you submit your website, discover five projects from other members, and earn discovery credits that place your website in front of real members around the world — no ad budget, no fees, no tricks.

Can I promote an app, a Telegram bot, a YouTube channel, an Instagram profile or a Facebook page?

Yes — that is the point of an all-in-one circle. Websites, SaaS and web apps, mobile apps, Telegram bots and channels, YouTube channels, Instagram profiles, Facebook pages, social profiles, open-source projects, podcasts and newsletters all live in the same circle, and members discover across all of them.

Is it really free? What is the catch?

It is free, and the exchange is the catch — an honest one. Instead of money, you contribute real attention: you genuinely discover other members’ projects, and the circle discovers yours. There are no plans and no pricing on GrowTogether — the whole circle is free, and nobody can buy priority inside it.

Is this a traffic exchange?

No. Traffic exchanges paid people in credits for opening pages without looking — worthless visits, and the model died. GrowTogether rewards genuine discovery and feedback, counts only sessions that pass integrity checks, and never forces anyone to install, like, subscribe or click an ad.

Will this bring me customers or a Google ranking?

No honest platform can promise that, and we will not be the ones who lie to you. What the circle guarantees is the thing money usually buys: real people discovering your project, real feedback, and a fair chance to be found. What your project does with that chance is yours.

When does it open?

First, an invite-only beta — so the loop, the fairness rules and the community are proven with real members before the doors open to the world. Waitlist members enter in order, and members who bring genuine builders through their invite link enter first.
